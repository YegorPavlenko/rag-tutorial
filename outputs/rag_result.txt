People comment on rumors in several ways, often defining them as an unsubstantiated report.
 When sharing information that might be a rumor, they frequently preface it with phrases like,
 I don't know if it is true or not, but people are saying... or acknowledge that A few people have mentioned that.

 There is also significant critical commentary regarding the practice of printing rumors as news or circulating rumors.
 People question news organizations when reporters concede claims are unsubstantiated but then proceed to summarize them.
 They distinguish between the existence of a report (which can be news) and the unsubstantiated content within it (which is a rumor),
 criticizing what they perceive as lack of reading or lack of comprehension, or spinning characterization when these lines are blurred.
